# Household Investment Policy Statement
**Adopted:** ____ 2026 · **Reviewed:** quarterly (numbers), annually (rules) · **Signed:** Yitz + Genesha

---

## Why this document exists

This portfolio is our only income. There is no paycheck behind it.

So its first job is not growth — it's making sure we are **never forced to sell something at a bad time to pay our bills**. That single risk is what could permanently damage us. Everything below is designed around preventing it.

We hold some concentrated positions we believe in (HYPE, AAPL, my father's firm). This document is the set of rules that earns us the right to keep holding them.

**The one rule behind all the others:** we don't change these rules in the middle of a scary market. Changes happen at the annual review, in writing, signed by both of us.

## 1. The Fortress (our safety buffer)

This is a big pile of boring, safe money that pays our bills for 30 months no matter what happens to everything else. All our spending comes out of here.

- **Target: 30 months of expenses** ($250k today), held in T-bills or SGOV — not idle cash.
- Every Evergreen distribution goes here first. Anything above target flows to the Diversified sleeve.
- **We never pay bills by deciding, under pressure, what to sell.** We pay bills from the Fortress and refill it on a schedule.
- *To do now: move the idle USAA cash into T-bills or SGOV — that's roughly $10k/year we're currently leaving on the table.*

## 2. How we refill the Fortress

These choices are decided in advance, so we're not making them while panicking.

- Each quarter, if the Fortress is under 30 months, refill from whatever is **up** — first HYPE sale proceeds, then flexible/Diversified holdings, then AAPL.
- **If nothing is up and the Fortress falls below 18 months:** we go to **austerity mode** — spending drops to **$75k/year** and we let the Fortress run down. We do not sell into a crash to refill it.
- **HYPE floor: $40.** Below $40, we never sell HYPE to pay for living expenses. Period.

## 3. HYPE — our biggest bet

We keep it, but we sell pieces on the way up automatically, so a big win turns into safety instead of just a bigger bet.

**Ceiling:** HYPE can't exceed **45% of liquid assets** (checked quarterly). If it does, we trim back to 40% within 30 days.

**The sell ladder** — these are pre-committed. When the price hits the number, we sell:

| Price | Tokens sold | Approx. proceeds |
|---|---|---|
| $100 | 250 | $25k |
| $125 | 500 | $62k |
| $150 | 1,000 | $150k |
| $175 | 1,500 | $262k |
| $200 | 1,500 | $300k |
| $225 | 1,000 | $225k |

**We keep 750 tokens forever, at any price.** These are our oldest, cheapest-basis tokens — never selling them avoids the tax entirely and keeps us in the story if HYPE goes somewhere extraordinary.

Selling all six rungs brings in about **$1.025M** before tax.

**We never buy more HYPE with Fortress or Diversified money.** Gains get skimmed; dips get held, not bought.

*Why rungs: the hardest one to follow will be the first. If we sell at $100 when everything feels euphoric, we'll follow the rest.*

## 4. Taxes on sales

Every sale creates a tax bill months before it's due. We set the money aside immediately so it's never a surprise.

- **Set aside 30% of every sale** into the Fortress, reserved for taxes. Only the remaining 70% goes to work.
- **The ladder always wins over tax optimization.** We never delay a sale to save on taxes. *Taxes are a cost; not selling is a risk — and risk rules beat cost rules.* (Only exception: a rung hitting in the last week of December may be split across the new year.)
- If a year gets big (over ~$500k in gains), raise the set-aside to **33%** on later sales that year — but don't change the schedule.
- Each sale means a quarterly estimated tax payment, paid from the set-aside.
- *Note: a big sale year likely costs us the child tax credit (phases out above $400k income).*
- **All tax numbers here are estimates. The CPA confirms actuals each year.**

## 5. ZEC — small bet

We like the thesis, but it's small on purpose — and there's a rule ready for what happens if it wins big.

- **If ZEC grows past 10% of liquid assets** at a quarterly review, trim back to 7%.
- No price ladder — this thesis isn't about a price target.
- ZEC counts inside the same **45% crypto ceiling** as HYPE. It doesn't protect us from a crypto crash; it falls in the same one.
- We only add with spare money, never by selling something else to buy it.

## 6. AAPL — the 20-year holding

We leave it alone, except when a low-income year lets us reduce it cheaply.

- We don't sell it to fund spending unless the refill rule (§2) calls for it.
- **In low-income years,** we can sell and immediately rebuy or diversify up to the top of the 0% or 15% tax bracket — this resets our cost basis nearly free. The CPA tells us how much room we have.
- Honest label: this is a **third concentrated bet**, not "our stock portfolio."

## 7. Diversified sleeve — the boring ballast

This is the part of the portfolio that isn't a bet. It's what protects us, and every dollar of new money goes here first.

- **Goal: at least 30% of liquid assets within 3 years.**
- Funded by: HYPE sale proceeds, extra Evergreen distributions, AAPL harvesting, and the current $100k flexible bucket.
- **Nothing exciting lives here** — no themes, no sectors, no crypto. If it moves with tech, it belongs in §8, not here.
- **What we hold:** **VOO** 55% (US large cap) · **VXUS** 30% (international) · **AVUV** 15% (US small-cap value). Three funds, all equities — the Fortress is our fixed income, so this sleeve doesn't need bonds.
- Rebalance with new money, not by selling. Every sale costs us ~30% in tax.
- **One thing to watch at each annual review:** the Fortress is a fixed dollar amount, so if the portfolio grows a lot it becomes a smaller share of what we own. If the Fortress ever drops below **15% of our liquid assets**, we revisit whether this sleeve should hold some bonds. Until then, the Fortress is our safety and this sleeve is our growth.
- When a big lump arrives: invest it within 30 days; if it's over $100k, in thirds.

## 8. Ascent sleeve — researched bets, capped

A small, walled-off space for investing in themes Yitz researches — capped so it can never hurt us.

- **Cap: 10% of liquid assets.** Over that, trim to 8%. No single position over 5% of liquid assets.
- **Funded only with spare money** — never from the Fortress or the Diversified sleeve.
- Every position needs a written thesis in our own words. *"The research said so" isn't a thesis.* If the thesis breaks, we sell regardless of price.
- **Changes only at quarterly reviews.** Research arrives weekly; we don't trade weekly. Each sale costs ~30% in tax, so turnover is expensive.
- Good research fills this bucket. It never makes the bucket bigger.

## 9. Evergreen — the family firm

- Valued conservatively. Not counted in our allocation math, never used as collateral.
- Distributions are **income, not capital.** We assume a year with zero distributions is possible — and survivable, because the Fortress covers full expenses.

## 10. The mortgage — $350k at 6.625%

Paying it off early is a guaranteed ~6.6% return — better than T-bills. But paid-down money can't be un-paid, and cash flexibility matters more to us than to most people. So it comes last, until it doesn't.

Payment is about **$2,240/month** — roughly a quarter of our annual spending.

**Where surplus money goes, in order:**
1. Fortress back to 30 months
2. Diversified sleeve on track
3. Whatever's left: **half to mortgage principal, half to Diversified**

**We switch to putting everything into the mortgage when any one of these happens:**
- Diversified sleeve reaches 30% of liquid assets, or
- The $150 HYPE rung sells, or
- Refinance rates are still above ~5.5% three years in.

*Why it matters: paying it off drops our annual spending from $100k to about $73k. That shrinks how big the Fortress needs to be and cuts our withdrawal rate by a quarter. For us, less debt is literally less risk — the mortgage is the one bill we can't cut in austerity mode.*

- After any prepayment of $50k or more, ask the lender for a **recast** (free, lowers the required monthly payment).
- If rates drop 1% or more below 6.625%, look at refinancing before prepaying more.
- **Never prepay from the Fortress.**

## 11. Things we will not do

- **We never borrow against the portfolio.**
- No new position over 5% of liquid assets. 48-hour waiting period on any trade over $25k.
- No selling HYPE below $40. No skipping a ladder rung because it "obviously" has further to run.
- No treating Evergreen or home equity as money we can spend.
- **No changing this document during a downturn.** Annual review only, in writing, both signatures.
